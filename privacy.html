Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Byteable (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Byteable app and website.

Information we collect

Account information: If you create an account, we may collect basic identifiers such as email address and user ID.

App data you enter: Foods, recipes, meals, macro targets, and related notes you choose to store.

Usage data: Basic analytics and diagnostic information (e.g., app version, crash logs) to improve reliability.

How we use information

  • Provide core app features (saving meals, tracking macros, syncing across devices)
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Improve performance and fix bugs
  • Communicate important service updates (when applicable)

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the app (for example, authentication, database hosting, analytics, crash reporting), and only as needed to provide the service.

Data retention

We retain data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion as described below.

Your choices

  • Access/Update: You can update your information within the app where available.
  • Deletion: You may request account and data deletion by contacting us.

Children’s privacy

Byteable is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us to request removal.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: byteable.app@gmail.com
